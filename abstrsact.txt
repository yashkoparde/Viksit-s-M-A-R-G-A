M.A.R.G.A is an enterprise-grade civic infrastructure intelligence platform designed to transform the governance, sanctioning, monitoring, social auditing, and inspection of public works executed under the Member of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS). Infrastructure funding under MPLADS currently faces severe operational bottlenecks. Official Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) findings in CAG Report No. 31 revealed a 27.3% unverified physical completion gap, where out of 3,913 reported complete works in Andhra Pradesh, only 2,843 were physically verified. Single-state ledger discrepancies have reached up to 29.5 Crore Rupees between reported expenditures and project ledgers. Executing agencies frequently submit inflated estimates exceeding Public Works Department (PWD) benchmarks or include disguised prohibited projects such as private property or religious structures under Annexure II guidelines. Scheme execution also suffers from duplicate funding across overlapping schemes, fake visual verification from gallery photo uploads, and unfocused physical inspections where statutory quotas (10% District Authority, 1% State) rely on random sampling across 13,000 sq km districts without targeting high-risk projects. M.A.R.G.A resolves these challenges by connecting six web portals, a native mobile app called MARGA Eyes, five machine learning engines called MARGA Brain, cryptographic audit trails, and national Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI).

M.A.R.G.A governs projects across five lifecycle stages: Pre-Sanction, Execution, Completion, Inspection, and Oversight. The platform unifies six specialized web portals. The MP Portal handles recommendation submission, constituency fund tracking, sector spend analytics, and automated recommendation letters. The District Authority Portal acts as the command center for AI proposal screening, cost benchmarking cards, digital sanctions, 90-day SLA tracking, and inspection route planning. The Implementing Agency Portal manages work orders, milestone payment claims, geotagged progress uploads, and automated GFR 12UC compilation. The State Nodal Officer Portal provides inter-district comparative audits, risk heatmaps, systemic inflation tracking, and agency rankings. The MoSPI National Portal offers macro dashboards monitoring national fund utilization, state compliance rankings, and policy metrics. The Public Transparency Portal provides an open citizen GIS map displaying project locations, budgets, executing agencies, site photos, and social audit feedback. Field verification is driven by MARGA Eyes, a native Android app built with CameraX API accessing raw camera hardware. It extracts hardware GPS, altitude, compass orientation, and timestamps at capture, enforcing a 15-meter geofence and blocking gallery uploads or location-spoofing tools. Rural areas support encrypted offline storage with automatic server synchronization.

The MARGA Brain microservice embeds five machine learning models executing on standard CPUs in under 10ms per request. Model 01 is the Inspection Priority Router, using Anomaly Risk Scoring and Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP) spatial clustering via NetworkX to evaluate progress logs, payment velocity, contractor history, and location. It identifies the top 10% highest-risk projects and clusters them into optimized itineraries, reducing District Collector inspection travel from 40 scattered trips to 15 optimized trips, achieving a 62.5% travel reduction. Model 02 is the PWD Cost and Delay Predictor, using LightGBM and TreeSHAP trained on historical PWD schedule of rates to evaluate proposed costs and timelines, generating explainable cost cards highlighting drivers like earthwork rates 35% above average. Model 03 is the 500m Geospatial Duplicate Radar, using Scikit-Learn BallTree spatial indexing with Haversine metrics to query new coordinates against 86,000+ national projects to block double-funding. Model 04 is the CameraX Geotag Authenticator, embedding hardware GPS coordinates, compass headings, and timestamps into image EXIF metadata for tamper-proof visual proof. Model 05 is the NLP Rule and Clause Classifier, using SentenceTransformers (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) semantic embeddings to check proposal text against Annexure II prohibited work categories in the MPLADS 2023 Guidelines.

M.A.R.G.A incorporates advanced integrity controls and government DPI integrations. The Dual-Lock Payment Guard automatically freezes fund disbursements if payout percentage outpaces physical progress by more than 15%. Cryptographic Audit Chaining seals every action, photo upload, sanction, and payment with SHA-256 hash chaining, where database editing breaks the chain. Citizen QR Code Audit Plaques allow citizens to scan site plaques with smartphones to view budget details, warranties, and lodge feedback directly into CPGRAMS. False Alarm Suppression (Z > 3 threshold) prevents alert fatigue by flagging implementing agencies only when performance strays beyond 3 standard deviations over their last 50 works. The platform integrates with national DPI stacks including PFMS/SNA 2.0 for account reconciliation, eSAKSHI for live MoSPI proposal feed sync, PM GatiShakti and ISRO NavIC for GIS mapping, Bhashini and WhatsApp Gateway for localized multilingual alerts, and Aadhaar e-Sign with DigiLocker for digital signatures. M.A.R.G.A requires zero policy changes as it automates existing statutory mandates. Cloud photo storage costs approximately 500 INR per month (7,200 INR per year for 270 GB), which is negligible compared to single-state ledger gaps of 29.5 Crore Rupees. Operationally, M.A.R.G.A eliminates the 27.3% physical verification gap, cuts inspection travel by 62.5%, reduces cost verification time from 90 days to less than 10ms, and lowers vehicle fuel emissions.
