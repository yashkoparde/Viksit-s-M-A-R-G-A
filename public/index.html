The Structural Gap

Eliminating the Multi-Department Paperwork Bottleneck

Historically, MPLADS projects suffered from unspent fund lapses, lack of geotagged visual proof, manual paperwork between MPs and District Authorities, and zero central oversight on project milestones. MARGA establishes a unified, tamper-proof verification chain:
